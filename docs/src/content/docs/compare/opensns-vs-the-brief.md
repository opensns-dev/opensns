# OpenSNS vs The Brief

Comparing The Brief against alternatives? You are probably weighing their feature set against the premium pricing and lack of flexibility. OpenSNS compares to The Brief across cost, control, and platform coverage.

## TL;DR Comparison

| Feature | OpenSNS | The Brief |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Pricing** | $0 to $98.99/mo | $29 to custom/seat |
| **Free tier** | 20 credits | None |
| **Self-hosting** | Yes (Docker, unlimited) | No |
| **Open source** | MIT License | Closed source |
| **Naver support** | 15+ platforms | None |
| **Video generation** | Yes (12 credits/video) | Yes (Sora 2, Veo 3.1) |
| **UGC avatars** | Yes (25 credits/video) | Yes |
| **Team seats** | Unlimited | 10 seats, then custom |

## Pricing Comparison

The Brief starts at $29 per month for basic features. Their Growth plan at $79 adds more capabilities. Team collaboration requires $49 per seat, which means a 10-person team pays $490 monthly before custom enterprise pricing kicks in.

OpenSNS takes a different approach. The free tier includes 20 credits. Paid plans range from $8.99 (145 credits) to $98.99 (1,980 credits). There are no per-seat charges. A 10-person team can share a single $28.99 Pro plan with 545 credits, or self-host for unlimited free usage.

For a 10-person team over one year, The Brief costs at least $5,880 ($49 x 10 seats x 12 months) plus base plan fees. OpenSNS costs $347.88 ($28.99 x 12) for the Pro plan, or $0 if self-hosted.

## Feature Deep-Dive

### Platform Support

The Brief supports major global platforms including META, Google Ads, Campaign Manager 360, and Display & Video 360. This covers enterprise advertising needs well.

OpenSNS supports global platforms including Instagram, Facebook, Google Ads, and TikTok. It also uniquely supports 15+ Naver platforms for the Korean market. If you run campaigns in Korea or need broader platform coverage, OpenSNS fills the gap The Brief leaves open.

### Control and Ownership

The Brief is closed source with no self-hosting option. Your data, brand assets, and campaign history live on their servers. The 2026 rebrand from Creatopy shows how platform direction can shift unexpectedly.

OpenSNS is MIT licensed open source. Self-host with Docker and everything stays on your infrastructure. Your API keys, campaign data, and generated assets remain under your control. You can audit the code, modify it, or fork it if needed.

### Template and Creative Libraries

The Brief offers 5,000+ templates and a comprehensive Brand Kit system (5 to unlimited brands depending on plan). This provides extensive starting points for creative teams.

OpenSNS includes a Template Library with 40+ templates and Brand Kit support. While smaller in raw numbers, OpenSNS templates integrate with its URL-to-campaign pipeline, automatically adapting to your product. The pluggable engine architecture lets you use any template system you prefer alongside the core platform.

### Team Collaboration

The Brief includes robust team features with 10 seats on standard plans, role-based access control, and automated A/B testing. This suits large teams with complex approval workflows.

OpenSNS supports unlimited team members without per-seat pricing. The approval workflow is built into the LangGraph agent pipeline, allowing strategy approval before generation. For smaller teams or agencies, OpenSNS eliminates seat-based friction while maintaining collaboration capabilities.

## Who Should Choose The Brief

The Brief makes sense if:

- You need 5,000+ templates and extensive creative libraries
- You require enterprise ad platform integrations (CM360, DV360)
- You have the budget for $49 per seat pricing
- You want built-in automated A/B testing without setup
- You need Feed AI Translate for multilingual campaigns
- Your team is large enough to justify seat-based pricing

The Brief suits enterprise teams with complex needs and budgets that accommodate premium pricing.

## Who Should Choose OpenSNS

OpenSNS is the better fit if:

- You want to eliminate per-seat pricing entirely
- You need support for Naver platforms or the Korean market
- You prefer open source with self-hosting options
- You want transparent, affordable pricing without enterprise sales
- You need UGC video generation without premium add-ons
- You value data ownership and want to avoid vendor lock-in

OpenSNS suits marketing teams, agencies, and technical users who want comprehensive features without enterprise costs.

## The Bottom Line

The Brief offers a wide feature set in a closed, premium package. OpenSNS delivers comparable core capabilities, including video and UGC, with the added benefits of open source, self-hosting, Naver support, and lower costs.

If you are tired of per-seat pricing, need Korean platform support, or want the freedom of open source, [try OpenSNS](/docs/getting-started/introduction/). Start free with 20 credits, or self-host for unlimited usage.

## FAQ

### Is OpenSNS free?

Yes. OpenSNS offers a free tier with 20 credits. You can also self-host the open-source version using Docker for unlimited free usage. Only the hosted cloud version requires payment.

### Can I self-host OpenSNS?

Yes. OpenSNS is MIT licensed and includes Docker one-click deployment. Self-hosting gives you unlimited free usage and full control over your data and API keys.

### Does The Brief support Naver platforms?

No. The Brief focuses on global platforms like META, Google Ads, CM360, and DV360. It does not support Naver platforms. OpenSNS uniquely supports 15+ Naver platforms including GFA.

### How does team pricing compare between OpenSNS and The Brief?

The Brief charges $49 per seat for team collaboration. A 10-person team costs $490 monthly plus base plan fees. OpenSNS has no per-seat charges. Teams can share any plan, or self-host for unlimited users at no cost.

### Does OpenSNS have templates like The Brief?

OpenSNS includes a Template Library with 40+ templates and Brand Kit support. While The Brief offers more templates (5,000+), OpenSNS templates integrate with its automated URL-to-campaign pipeline and pluggable engine architecture for greater flexibility.
