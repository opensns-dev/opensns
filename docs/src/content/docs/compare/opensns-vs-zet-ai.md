# OpenSNS vs Zet AI

Evaluating Zet AI against alternatives? You have probably noticed their pricing is not public and their approach differs from typical generative AI tools. OpenSNS compares to Zet AI across transparency, technology, and platform support.

## TL;DR Comparison

| Feature | OpenSNS | Zet AI |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Pricing** | $0 to $98.99/mo (transparent) | Sales-based (undisclosed) |
| **Free tier** | 20 credits | Unknown |
| **AI approach** | Generative AI (creates new) | Vision-based (transforms existing) |
| **Self-hosting** | Yes (Docker, unlimited) | No |
| **Open source** | MIT License | Closed source |
| **Platform support** | 15+ Naver + global | META, Google, GFA only |
| **Team size** | Any | 500+ agency focus |

## Pricing Comparison

Zet AI does not publish pricing. As a Korean company serving 500+ agency customers with only a 2-person team, they likely operate on custom enterprise contracts. This typically means higher costs, minimum commitments, and sales calls before you can evaluate the product.

OpenSNS publishes all pricing upfront. The free tier includes 20 credits. Paid plans range from $8.99 (145 credits) to $98.99 (1,980 credits). There are no sales calls, no minimums, and you can start immediately. Self-hosting is free forever.

For agencies managing multiple clients, OpenSNS transparent pricing makes budgeting predictable. Zet AI custom pricing may work for large enterprises with dedicated budgets, but it creates friction for smaller teams.

## Feature Deep-Dive

### AI Technology Approach

Zet AI uses a vision-based approach. They analyze competitor ads and transform them into ads for your product. This means your output is derivative of what competitors are already running. It works well for following market trends but limits originality.

OpenSNS uses generative AI. It researches your product URL, analyzes competitors for strategy insights, then creates entirely new copy, images, and videos. You get fresh creative angles rather than variations of existing ads. The 40 concept presets in Zet AI are templates. OpenSNS generates unique concepts based on your specific product and strategy.

### Platform Support

Zet AI supports META, Google Ads, and GFA (Naver). This covers the major platforms but stops there. If you need other Naver properties or global platforms like TikTok, you are out of luck.

OpenSNS supports 15+ Naver platforms including GFA, plus global platforms like Instagram, Facebook, Google Ads, and TikTok. For Korean marketers who need comprehensive Naver coverage, or global marketers who need multi-platform support, OpenSNS provides broader reach.

### Data Ownership and Control

Zet AI is closed source with no self-hosting option. Your campaign data, competitor analysis, and creative assets live on their servers. With only a 2-person team and 200 million KRW in VC funding, long-term stability depends on their continued funding and acquisition potential.

OpenSNS is MIT licensed open source. Self-host with Docker and your data never leaves your infrastructure. Use the cloud version and you still own your API keys and can export everything. There is no vendor lock-in.

### Scalability and Team Size

Zet AI focuses on agencies with 500+ customers. Their feature set and pricing likely target this segment. Smaller teams or individual marketers may find the platform oriented toward enterprise workflows.

OpenSNS scales from solo marketers to large agencies. The free tier works for individuals. The $8.99 Basic plan suits small teams. The $98.99 Ultra plan with 1,980 credits handles agency volume. Self-hosting removes all limits entirely.

## Who Should Choose Zet AI

Zet AI makes sense if:

- You are a Korean agency already embedded in their ecosystem
- You prefer vision-based ad transformation over generative creation
- You have the budget for custom enterprise pricing
- You only need META, Google, and GFA support
- You want a managed service from a small, focused team

Zet AI suits large Korean agencies comfortable with sales-led procurement and derivative creative approaches.

## Who Should Choose OpenSNS

OpenSNS is the better fit if:

- You want transparent, published pricing with no sales calls
- You prefer generative AI that creates original content
- You need support for 15+ Naver platforms or global platforms like TikTok
- You want the option to self-host for unlimited free usage
- You value open source and data ownership
- You are a small team, agency, or individual marketer

OpenSNS suits marketers who want control, transparency, and comprehensive platform support without enterprise pricing.

## The Bottom Line

Zet AI offers a vision-based approach for Korean agencies willing to navigate sales-led pricing. OpenSNS delivers generative AI, transparent pricing, broader platform support, and the flexibility of open source.

If you prefer knowing costs upfront, creating original content rather than transforming competitor ads, or need platforms beyond Zet AI limited set, [try OpenSNS](/docs/getting-started/introduction/). Start free with 20 credits, or self-host for unlimited usage.

## FAQ

### Is OpenSNS free?

Yes. OpenSNS offers a free tier with 20 credits. You can also self-host the open-source version using Docker for unlimited free usage. Only the hosted cloud version requires payment.

### Can I self-host OpenSNS?

Yes. OpenSNS is MIT licensed and includes Docker one-click deployment. Self-hosting gives you unlimited free usage and full control over your data and API keys.

### What is the difference between Zet AI vision-based approach and OpenSNS generative AI?

Zet AI analyzes competitor ads and transforms them into ads for your product. OpenSNS researches your product and competitors, then generates entirely new creative concepts. OpenSNS produces original content while Zet AI creates derivative variations.

### Does Zet AI publish their pricing?

No. Zet AI uses sales-based pricing that is not publicly disclosed. You must contact their team for a quote. OpenSNS publishes all pricing upfront with no sales calls required.

### Which Naver platforms does OpenSNS support?

OpenSNS supports 15+ Naver platforms including GFA and other local ad networks. Zet AI only supports GFA among Naver properties. For comprehensive Korean market coverage, OpenSNS offers broader support.
